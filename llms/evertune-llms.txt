# Evertune | Improve Your Brand’s Visibility in AI Search

Evertune is a comprehensive intelligence platform that helps brands, marketers, and agencies understand, measure, and influence how their brand appears in AI-driven recommendations. As discovery shifts from traditional search engines to conversational answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, Evertune enables organizations to track their AI visibility, analyze how LLMs describe them, and take action to improve it. The platform combines large-scale prompt testing, narrative analytics, and prescriptive insights to help brands stay discoverable, accurately represented, and competitive as AI reshapes how people learn about and choose products.
